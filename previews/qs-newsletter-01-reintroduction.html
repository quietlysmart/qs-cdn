Hi there,

It has probably been a while since you heard from me. For some of you, quite a long while.

A quick reintroduction

You are receiving this because our paths crossed through my work, a project, an event or something I shared online. You may remember Quietly Smart. You may not. Either is fair.

I’m Matt Wills. I run Quietly Smart, where I help founders, small teams and creative businesses use AI in ways that are useful, controlled and worth the effort. Most of what I write comes from work I’m doing myself, including the parts that break.

Why I am writing

I’m starting a short newsletter, sent once every two weeks. It’s about using AI with judgement: what’s worth trying, what isn’t, and how to make it useful in a real business. No tool dumps, launch-day hype or pretending every new model changes everything.

What every issue will contain

One useful idea. A practical lesson you can apply to your own work, explained without assuming you are technical.

One honest example. Something I have built, tested or learned from real work, including what failed and what I changed.

Two or three developments worth knowing. Not copied headlines. I will explain what changed, why you might care and when you can safely ignore it.

Most issues should take about four minutes to read. If I have nothing useful to say that fortnight, I would rather skip one than fill your inbox for the sake of a schedule.

The choice is yours

The last thing I want is to become another person you barely remember, sending you email you did not ask for. If the promise above sounds useful, tell me to keep you on the list. If it does not, unsubscribe now. No awkward follow-up.

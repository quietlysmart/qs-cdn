Imagine quoting for a job that should take two days. The price looks fine, but what if it takes three? You can now ask ChatGPT to build an interactive calculator right there on screen, inside your conversation. Change the numbers in the tool and see what happens.

Try changing the numbers

OpenAI's 7 October update lets ChatGPT create interactive tools with buttons, charts and editable inputs. The calculator appears on screen inside ChatGPT, and you use it there in the same conversation.

If the feature is available in your Chat tab, describe the decision and the numbers you'd like to change. Ask ChatGPT to build an interactive calculator you can use directly in the chat. You should be able to edit the inputs on screen and see the result update.

For example, imagine a job paying $1,200, with $60 in travel costs. You expect it to take 18 hours. After travel, that leaves $63.33 an hour, before tax and any other costs. If it takes 24 hours, that falls to $47.50. The price hasn't changed, but you might feel quite differently about accepting it.

You can ask for something like this:

Copy-paste promptBuild an interactive calculator that appears on screen and works inside this ChatGPT conversation for deciding whether a job is worth taking. Give me editable fields for the fee, hours and travel costs, and update the result when I change them. Use US dollars. Start with a $1,200 fee, 18 hours and $60 travel. Show what is left per hour after travel, using (fee minus travel) divided by hours. Label it before tax and other costs. Keep the formula visible. Then let me change the hours to 24.

After ChatGPT builds it, try changing the fields in the tool instead of asking the same question again. What fee would make the longer job worthwhile? What if you need a second trip? You're still making the decision, but you have something easier to work with than a paragraph telling you it depends.

Check one calculation yourself before using it. In this example, $1,140 divided by 24 should give $47.50. Check that the tool is using the formula you asked for, with your figures in the right places.

OpenAI calls this Intelligent UI. It began rolling out on 7 October in ChatGPT's Chat tab, first to paid plans and then Free and Go. Enterprise access depends on workplace settings, so it may not appear for everyone yet. This update applies to ChatGPT's Chat tab. It works in Chat, but not with the reasoning setting labelled Pro.
